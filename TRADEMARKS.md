# Rotiva name and logo policy

Rotiva's **source code** is licensed under [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name "Rotiva"**, the
**Rotiva logo** (the orange "Orbit" arrow with a center dot), and the **Rotiva app icon** are not covered
by that license. They belong to Denish Chairany and identify the official app. GPL-3.0 section 7(e) allows
this.

The goal is simple: people should always know whether they're getting the official Rotiva or something
built from it.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that your project is **based on**, **forked from**, or **compatible with** Rotiva.
- Sharing **unmodified** official installers from
  [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/uncrownedk1ng2812/rotiva/releases) under the Rotiva name, for
  example on download sites or package managers, as long as you keep the license files.
- Using the name and logo to **write about** Rotiva, in reviews, articles, videos, tutorials and so on.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Publishing a **modified** version (fork) under the name "Rotiva", or under a confusingly similar
  name such as "Rotiva Pro", "Rotiva Plus" or "Rotiva2".
- Using the Rotiva logo or icon, or a look-alike, for a fork or for another product.
- Suggesting that your fork, product or service is official or endorsed by Denish Chairany.

## If you fork Rotiva

1. Choose a different name and a different logo/icon.
2. Keep the GPL-3.0 license and the copyright notice, and publish your full source code.
3. Credit the original:
   `Based on Rotiva by Denish Chairany — https://github.com/uncrownedk1ng2812/rotiva`

Questions or permission requests: <https://github.com/uncrownedk1ng2812>
