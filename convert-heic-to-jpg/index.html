How to Convert HEIC to JPG on Windows Offline (iPhone Photos, Free)

iPhone photos are saved as HEIC, which many Windows apps, upload forms and print shops can't open. Rotiva converts them to JPG on your own PC, dozens at a time.

How to do it (3 steps)

  1. Select one or many HEIC photos and start dragging them.
  2. Press Shift while you keep holding the mouse button.
  3. Point at JPG and let go. The JPG copies are saved in the same folder.

Frequently asked questions

Will the photo lose quality?

JPG is lossy, but for everyday use the difference is hard to see. Pick PNG if you want no further loss.

Are the original HEIC files changed?

No. Rotiva saves new files next to the originals.
