# Changelog

## 1.11.0

### New on the wheel
- **EDIT**: a second wheel page. Images: rotate, flip, grayscale. Videos: rotate, flip, reverse (up to 30 s), and a 16-frame contact sheet. Audio: even out the loudness, reverse, save the cover art.
- **BURN IN**: drag one video and one subtitle file together to put the subtitles on the picture.
- **LOCK** (Windows): an AES-256 ZIP with a password, from the wheel or the right-click menu. Passwords use plain Latin letters, numbers and common symbols. Open it with 7-Zip or WinRAR.
- **SHEET**: several photos in one picture.

### New on Windows
- **Convert clipboard**: copy files or an image, or take a screenshot, then press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V or use the tray menu.
- **Guide**: step-by-step help from the tray menu.

### More formats
- Images: PSD, SVG, JPEG XL, JPEG 2000, TGA, EXR, HDR, DDS, QOI and camera RAW (DNG, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, ORF, RW2, RAF and more).
- Video: MPG, VOB, M2TS, OGV, ASF, MXF, RM and more.
- Audio: M4B, AMR, APE, WavPack, DSF, AC3, DTS and more, with AAC and AIFF as new targets.
- New categories: **E-books** (EPUB, FB2), **Subtitles** (SRT, VTT, ASS, SSA, SMI, LRC) and **Data** (JSON, YAML, CSV).
- Documents can become EPUB; spreadsheets can become JSON.

### Changed
- Your clipboard image is saved in `Pictures\Rotiva` while it converts.
- The right-click menu lists the new tools.
